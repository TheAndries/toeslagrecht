Status
open — a published method, not yet a decision; the encoding follows the text. To be classified when a beschikking or a reading of the text that covers it appears. 2026-10-09: the Dienst's Berekening zorgtoeslag 2024 (stap 5 onder c) gave for the same situation a third formula, "(2x standaardpremie x woonlandfactor – normpremie) x 50%", i.e. the factor on both premies; the Dienst's method changed between 2024 and 2026 while the text (then art. 3, now art. 4a) did not on this point. Art. 4a lid 3 (verzekerde with a verdragsgerechtigde partner) exists only since 06-11-2024 (Stb. 2024, 291); for 2024 the encoding returns undetermined for that case. 2026-10-11: the Dienst's Berekening zorgtoeslag 2023 (stap 5) gives the same four formulas as 2024, onder c identical ("(2x standaardpremie x woonlandfactor – normpremie) x 50%"), and onder d for a partner who is a verzekerde but not a verdragsgerechtigde: "(standaardpremie x woonlandfactor + standaardpremie) – normpremie", which is what art. 3 lid 3 (2023 text; now art. 4a lid 4) says. So the method of 2023 and 2024 was one method, changed by 2026; the text of 2023 equals that of 2024 on this point (art. 3). 2026-10-12: the Dienst's Berekening zorgtoeslag 2022 (stap 5) has the same four formulas a–d as 2023 and 2024, onder c identical ("(2x standaardpremie x woonlandfactor – normpremie) x 50%"), and its rekenvoorbeeld 3 applies it (€ 3.498 × 0,8138 for a verdragsgerechtigde with a partner who is neither verzekerde nor verdragsgerechtigde) — the only worked example of this formula in the five leaflets; the encoding passes it on the standaardpremie (zt-2022-pub-003), because for a verdragsgerechtigde aanvrager with such a partner the text (art. 3 lid 3, 2022 text; now art. 4a lid 4) and the Dienst's onder c agree that both premies carry the factor. The difference of this entry arises only for a verzekerde aanvrager (zt-2026-syn-001), a case no leaflet works out. So the method of 2022, 2023 and 2024 was one method, changed by 2026
