Status
government — classified 2026-10-11: the encoding follows the text, which rounds nothing but the year amount (Awir art. 14 lid 4); the Dienst's printed method truncated cents in its 2023 leaflet and rounded them from 2024 on. No granted amount is affected: the whole-euro month (€ 154, € 113) and the year amount are the same under both. Recorded because the record is the point: the Dienst's arithmetic changed between two editions of the same leaflet while the text did not. Not #1 (a different cause: cent truncation, not the drempelinkomen) and not #5 (the whole-euro month, which the 2023 leaflet forms as #5 says: 154,84 → 154; 113,90 → 113). 2026-10-12: the 2022 leaflet (TG 082 - 1Z21FD, januari 2022) is mixed: it rounds the normpremie (1,848% × € 22.356 = € 413,13888 → "€ 413,14"; € 2.420,4094 → "€ 2.420,41") and the 50% step of rekenvoorbeeld 2 (€ 538,795 → "€ 538,80"), but truncates the 50% step of rekenvoorbeeld 3 (€ 1.902,13 × 50% = € 951,065 → "€ 951,06") and the month in both examples that have a fractional cent (€ 44,8996 → "€ 44,89"; € 79,2554 → "€ 79,25"). It also misprints the subtotal of rekenvoorbeeld 3 as "€ 1.602,13" where € 2.846,67 − € 944,54 = € 1.902,13 (the 50% figure confirms 1.902,13; a data error of the kind of #4, kept in the case as published). New cases under this entry: zt-2022-pub-002 (month cent) and zt-2022-pub-003 (year and month cents). So the series reads: 2022 mixed, 2023 truncated throughout, 2024–2026 rounded throughout — three printed methods in five editions, with no text behind any of them; no whole-euro amount is affected
