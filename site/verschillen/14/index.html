CHANGELOG.md 2026-11-01. The Dienst's € 561 follows exactly if the normpremie is first put in whole cents (€ 1.188,50): € 1.749 − € 1.188,50 = € 560,50 → € 561. No article provides for rounding the normpremie; Wzt art. 2 lid 3 defines it as a sum of percentages and Awir art. 14 lid 4 rounds the tegemoetkoming, once. The 2022 leaflet computes with intermediate cents (#7), which is the same method; this is the first decision in the record where that method moves a whole euro — the margin is 0,06 cent. Same shape as #1 and #10: an intermediate rounding the text does not have.
