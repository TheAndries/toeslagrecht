government — classified 2026-10-08 after a full-text search of the Wzt, the Awir and the Uitvoeringsregeling Awir found no rounding of the drempelinkomen; Ask 5(a) stays open for any text the project missed. 2026-10-09: the 2024 leaflet says "vastgesteld op € 26.819" where the text gives € 26.819,424 — rounded down that year, up in 2025 (€ 28.405,728 → € 28.406) and up in 2026 (€ 29.735,424 → € 29.736, where arithmetic rounding would give € 29.735). The Dienst's rounding is thus not one rule across years. New cases under this entry: zt-2024-pub-001..003 (cents: normpremie € 503,93 / € 2.013,70 / € 1.141,42 by the Dienst, € 503,94 / € 2.013,66 / € 1.141,43 by the text) and zt-2026-tab-mp-44500 (outcome: € 935,46 → € 935 → € 77 per month by the text; € 935,53 → € 936 → € 78 by the Dienst's drempelinkomen, as the table publishes). 2026-10-10: the owner's answer to Ask 5(a), from a decade of financial practice (evidence, not authority; BOARD.md), verbatim in CHANGELOG.md 2026-10-10: no written rule known to him; in practice an amount the citizen pays is rounded down and an amount the citizen receives is rounded up, while thresholds are kept at two decimals as published. Read against the record: the drempelinkomen is a threshold, and the Dienst rounds it to whole euros (not two decimals), down in 2024 and up in 2025 and 2026; the monthly amount is an amount received, and the Dienst rounds it down (#5); the year amount is rounded arithmetically by a written rule (Awir art. 14 lid 4). The practice rule as stated therefore explains none of the three observed roundings; the entry stays government. 2026-10-11: the 2023 leaflet (TG 082 - 1Z32FD, januari 2023) says "vastgesteld op € 25.070" where the text gives € 25.069,824 (108% × 12 × € 1.934,40, Stb. 2022, 381) — rounded up, as in 2025 and 2026; 2024 remains the only year rounded down.
