government — classified 2026-10-08 after a full-text search of the Wzt, the Awir and the Uitvoeringsregeling Awir found no rounding of the drempelinkomen; Ask 5(a) stays open for any text the project missed. 2026-10-09: the 2024 leaflet says "vastgesteld op € 26.819" where the text gives € 26.819,424 — rounded down that year, up in 2025 (€ 28.405,728 → € 28.406) and up in 2026 (€ 29.735,424 → € 29.736, where arithmetic rounding would give € 29.735). The Dienst's rounding is thus not one rule across years. New cases under this entry: zt-2024-pub-001..003 (cents: normpremie € 503,93 / € 2.013,70 / € 1.141,42 by the Dienst, € 503,94 / € 2.013,66 / € 1.141,43 by the text) and zt-2026-tab-mp-44500 (outcome: € 935,46 → € 935 → € 77 per month by the text; € 935,53 → € 936 → € 78 by the Dienst's drempelinkomen, as the table publishes). 2026-10-10: the owner's answer to Ask 5(a), from a decade of financial practice (evidence, not authority; BOARD.md), verbatim in CHANGELOG.md 2026-10-10: no written rule known to him; in practice an amount the citizen pays is rounded down and an amount the citizen receives is rounded up, while thresholds are kept at two decimals as published. Read against the record: the drempelinkomen is a threshold, and the Dienst rounds it to whole euros (not two decimals), down in 2024 and up in 2025 and 2026; the monthly amount is an amount received, and the Dienst rounds it down (#5); the year amount is rounded arithmetically by a written rule (Awir art. 14 lid 4). The practice rule as stated therefore explains none of the three observed roundings; the entry stays government. 2026-10-11: the 2023 leaflet (TG 082 - 1Z32FD, januari 2023) says "vastgesteld op € 25.070" where the text gives € 25.069,824 (108% × 12 × € 1.934,40, Stb. 2022, 381) — rounded up, as in 2025 and 2026; 2024 remains the only year rounded down. New case under this entry: zt-2023-pub-002 (cents: normpremie € 1.705,09 and aanspraak € 1.036,45 by the Dienst, € 1.705,12 and € 1.036,44 by the text). zt-2023-pub-001 and -003 have incomes at or below the drempelinkomen and show no difference from this cause (their cents are #7). The 2023 ceilings (€ 38.520 / € 48.224) coincide under both drempelinkomens (zt-2023-pub-004..007). 2026-10-12: in 2022 the drempelinkomen is a whole amount by the text itself (108% × 12 × € 1.725,00 = € 22.356,00; Stcrt. 2021, 44177) and the 2022 leaflet says "vastgesteld op € 22.356": no rounding occurred, so 2022 is evidence of nothing here; the 2022 ceilings (€ 31.998 / € 40.944) follow from the text (zt-2022-pub-004..007). 2026-10-23: for 2015 (drempelinkomen € 19.463,328 by the text; no Dienst figure, leaflet not found) the direction of the rounding would decide the ceiling zonder partner: rounded down to € 19.463 the text's ceilings (€ 26.316 / € 32.655) stand; rounded up to € 19.464 — as the Dienst did in 2023, 2025 and 2026 — the ceiling zonder partner moves to € 26.317 (€ 23,54 → € 24) while met partner it stays (zt-2015-syn-032..035, computed from the text and verified through the encoding). The one 2015 Dienst outcome with an income, € 663 at € 27.886 met partner (zt-2015-rul-001), is the same whole euro under all three.
