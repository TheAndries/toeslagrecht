## Not yet consulted (open items, in priority order)

1. Wzt art. 2 lid 6: the wetstechnische informatie of art. 2 lists only the Besluit percentages as gedelegeerde regelgeving (2026-10-09), so no ministerial regeling on the monthly determination exists under it; the 2024 text of lid 6 refers to "het zesde lid" (itself).
