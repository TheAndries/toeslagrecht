# GRANT.md

The funding plan. Grants are fuel for Phase 3 onward (`PLAN.md`), never the goal, and never
spent outside their own budget (`CHARTER.md`, Money).

## Prerequisites before any application

- One toeslag encoded end to end, every rule tested, at least one year of history.
- At least 50 verified cases and a public discrepancy log with entries in it.
- At least one `reviewed` rule from a named practitioner who is not the owner.
- A live checker that has been used by people the owner did not ask.

An application without these is a pitch. With them it is an exhibit.

## Funders to verify

The operator verifies current programmes, rounds, amounts and conditions before drafting,
and records them here with the date checked. Starting points, not facts:

- **SIDN fonds** — internet for the public good; small and larger grants.
- **NLnet** (NGI programmes) — open-source public infrastructure; pays per delivered
  milestone, which suits an agent-run project.
- **Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie** — digital culture, where the design and the
  public-information angle fit.
- Ministerial or provincial innovation calls — only if a specific call names this kind of
  work.

## The budget, in principle

Honest and modest. Lines, in the order a funder will read them:

1. **Compute** — the project's own tokens and hosting, costed from `LEDGER.md` history.
2. **The owner's hours** — only the human-only work: reading rules against articles,
   anonymising and verifying cases, talking to practitioners and institutions, the
   funder's interview, reporting. At a rate a public-interest funder accepts without
   comment, not the owner's commercial rate.
3. **A fiscalist's review** — a contractor to review each toeslag's encoding before it is
   marked `reviewed` on the site.
4. **Accessibility audit** — one external check against `DESIGN.md` §6.

Grant money is accounted in `LEDGER.md` under its own heading and spent only on these lines.
Money for the owner's hours is the owner's, once paid, and is not the project's concern.

## Who does what

The operator drafts the application, the budget and every report, from the record. The
owner reads, corrects, signs, and sits the interview. The operator never contacts a funder.
